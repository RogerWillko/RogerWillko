I build tools that leave **evidence**: human-checked work records, private night probes for model names, custody protocols for weights, and local-first apps that keep your data on the device. Official enough to invoice under my real name. Honest enough to say what a system does not do.



---

### Featured work

**[Scrollstime](https://scrollstime.online)** — A public record of work someone actually checked. You submit what you built; a person opens it, checks that it is yours and that it does what you said. If it holds up, you get a certificate and a public page. It expires after a year. Nothing is issued automatically.

**[Night Grade](https://scrollstime.com)** — Same private questions, night after night, against model names you watch. The board is meant to post percents and fall alerts; questions and replies stay off the public host. The [method](https://scrollstime.com/method.html) is written up. Live board nights are not published yet — the public page shows the method and specimen layout until real nights are posted.

**[Witness Ledger](https://github.com/RogerWillko/witness-ledger)** — A witness-and-consequences protocol for model weights. The actor does not hold the record. Wrapper, judge, and witness have separate jobs. Prototype. MIT. Not an alignment system.

**[Dayring](https://github.com/RogerWillko/dayring)** — Local-first Flutter app for the day you are in: schedule, budget, goals, and scarce collector rings. Nothing leaves the phone. No account. No sync.

**[Pantry Pattern / Spaghettify](https://pantrypattern.grok.me)** — Pattern recognition through a black hole. Spaghettification is a feature.
